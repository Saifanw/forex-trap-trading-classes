Loss hua hai? Aap akele nahi hain.

Trading mein loss hona stressful ho sakta hai—khaaskar jab aapko lagta hai ki abhi loss ko wapas lana hai. Lekin loss ko turant recover karne ki koshish mein lot size badhana, overtrading ya bina setup ke entry lena risk ko aur badha sakta hai.

Important: Koi bhi trader ya educator loss recovery, fixed profit, guaranteed return ya capital wapas milne ki guarantee nahi de sakta. Is page ka purpose educational review aur risk-awareness hai—not a promise to recover money.

Loss ke baad 4-step reset

1. Pause karein

Emotional ya revenge trading se bachne ke liye break lein. Aaj trade na karna bhi valid decision hai.

2. Trades review karein

Entry, stop-loss, position size aur rule-following ko journal mein likhein. Sirf result nahi, process dekhein.

3. Risk dobara set karein

Per-trade risk aur daily loss limit pehle decide karein. Loss ke baad risk/lot size badha kar chase na karein.

4. Plan ke saath hi aage badhein

Setup, invalidation aur stop-loss clear na ho toh trade skip karein. Zaroorat ho toh qualified financial professional se salah lein.
